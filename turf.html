About the Ground

Professional Grade
From the Ground Up

Built to international standards, our synthetic wicket and outfield deliver the same playing experience as professional facilities. With 500 LUX LED floodlighting and a meticulously maintained surface, Penalty Box Turf is the premier venue for competitive cricket in the city.

Spanning a full playing area with high bounce safety netting, our facility accommodates all formats of bounded cricket. The drainage system is the best in the city — rain or shine, the match goes on.

Every Amenity Covered

Premium facilities at every touchpoint of your visit — from arrival to recovery.

Pro-Grade Surface

Synthetic monofilament wicket used globally. Consistent ball bounce and predictable seam every session.

LED Floodlights

Shadow-free 500 LUX lighting system. 8 tower-mounted arrays ensure zero dark spots anywhere on the ground.

Private Parking

Secured camera-monitored parking for 50 vehicles. Free with every booking. Located directly adjacent to the facility entrance.

Locker Rooms

Temperature-controlled changing rooms with hot showers, secure lockers, and clean towel service.

Refreshment Zone

Fully stocked hydration station, sports drinks, energy snacks, and a relaxed post-match lounge area.

Spectator Seating

600-capacity covered stands with elevated sightlines. Perfect for league matches and supporters.

All-Weather Drainage

Best drainage system in the city. Engineered to be game-ready 30 minutes after heavy rainfall.

High-Speed WiFi

Free gigabit WiFi throughout the facility for live scoring, live streaming, and communications.

Match Recording

Optional 4K fixed-camera match recording to capture your best boundaries and wickets.
