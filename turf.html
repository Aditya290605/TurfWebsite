🏟️ About the Pitch

Professional Grade
From the Ground Up

Built to FIFA Pro standards, our synthetic monofilament turf delivers the same playing experience as professional club facilities. With 500 LUX LED floodlighting and a meticulously maintained surface, Elite Turf Arena is the premier venue for amateur and semi-professional football in the city.

Spanning a full 105 × 68 metre pitch, our facility accommodates 11-a-side, 7-a-side, and 5-a-side configurations. The drainage system is the best in the city — rain or shine, the game goes on.

Every Amenity Covered

Premium facilities at every touchpoint of your visit — from arrival to recovery.

🏟️

FIFA Quality Turf

Synthetic monofilament grass used by professional clubs globally. Consistent ball roll and predictable bounce every session.

💡

LED Floodlights

Shadow-free 500 LUX lighting system. 8 tower-mounted arrays ensure zero dark spots anywhere on the pitch.

🅿️

Private Parking

Secured camera-monitored parking for 50 vehicles. Free with every booking. Located directly adjacent to the facility entrance.

🚿

Locker Rooms

Temperature-controlled changing rooms with hot showers, secure lockers, and clean towel service.

🥤

Refreshment Zone

Fully stocked hydration station, sports drinks, energy snacks, and a relaxed post-game lounge area.

🪑

Spectator Seating

600-capacity covered stands with elevated sightlines. Perfect for league matches and coaching staff.

🌧️

All-Weather Drainage

Best drainage system in the city. Engineered to be game-ready 30 minutes after heavy rainfall.

📶

High-Speed WiFi

Free gigabit WiFi throughout the facility for coaching analysis, live streaming, and communications.

📹

Match Recording

Optional 4K fixed-camera match recording with post-game highlight package available for all bookings.
